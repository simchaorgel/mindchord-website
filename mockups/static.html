The texture stays upstairs

Here the noise is a hero treatment rather than an atmosphere. It does its work above the fold and then hands over completely — the copy sits on flat green with nothing competing behind it.

The argument for this version is focus. Text on a solid field is easier to read, the page gets quieter as you descend, and the texture keeps its impact precisely because it does not follow you around.

The argument against is that the site stops feeling like one object. The hero becomes a banner sitting on top of a different, plainer page, and whatever character the texture gave you is gone by the second screen.

The seam is where this lives or dies. This version cuts hard — no fade, no blend, the texture simply stops. That only works if the flat green sits at the same value as the texture above it, otherwise the cut reads as a brightness fault rather than a decision.
