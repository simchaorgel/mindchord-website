The texture settles down here

Above the fold the noise runs at a low frequency — big, dramatic, molten blobs that fill the screen and do the work of a hero image without being one.

As you scroll it sweeps continuously toward a much higher frequency. The same green, the same technique, but the features shrink to a fine mottle that behaves like a background instead of competing with the words sitting on top of it.

Nothing is recomputed while you scroll. In scale mode a single texture is magnified — and because magnifying noise is arithmetically the same as lowering its frequency, the sweep is genuinely continuous rather than a set of cross-fades.

Scroll position is also damped: the texture eases toward where you are rather than tracking the wheel one-to-one, which removes the jitter that input alone would introduce.
