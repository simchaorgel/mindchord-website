What to look for

Flip "mode" to compare. In old mode the hero has magenta and yellow speckles because the raw turbulence colour is painted over the green. In new mode the noise is grayscale and blended, so it only pushes the green lighter and darker, and the radial highlight in the top left becomes visible.

Resize the window in old mode and the panels re-rasterize every time. In new mode the tile is rendered once and cached.

Textured panels carry the statements. Flat sections carry the reading.

Bands

The two bands now use different seeds, so they are no longer the same patch of noise. They also run 4 octaves instead of 6. The dropped octaves were at 0.32 and 0.64 cycles per pixel, finer than a pixel, so nothing visible is lost.

The hero toggle switches between fractalNoise (soft clouds) and turbulence (sharp veins). Both are the tiled, blended version.

The same material at a different grain — loud enough to mark a section, quiet enough to read across.
second band · seed 29

Tiling

The hero tile is 1024px and the band tiles are 512px. At baseFrequency 0.005 the hero blobs are about 200px across, so on a very wide screen the tile repeats a couple of times. If that ever reads as a pattern, raise the tile to 2048.
