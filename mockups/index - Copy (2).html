First section

Body copy sits on flat green with nothing behind it. The hero has already done the loud work and stopped dead — no fade, no blend, the panel simply ends.

The flat green is deliberately darker than the texture it cuts against. That step is what makes the edge read as a decision rather than a near-miss, and it buys a little text contrast at the same time.

Textured panels carry the statements. Flat sections carry the reading.

Second section

Bands appear occasionally — twice on a typical page, more on a long one — and never so often that one is always in view. The texture returns as a punctuation mark, not as atmosphere.

Because each band rasterizes its own filter, the cost scales with how many you use and how tall they are. Two or three per page is comfortable. A band behind every section would be both slower and less effective.

The same material at a different grain — loud enough to mark a section, quiet enough to read across.

Closing section

To add another band anywhere on the page, write <section class="band"> and put statement type inside it. Nothing else is required — no wrapper element, no script, no per-section configuration.
