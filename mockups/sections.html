The texture becomes a system

The hero does the loud work and then stops dead. No fade, no blend — the panel simply ends and the page continues on flat green, so the copy has nothing competing behind it.

What keeps the site feeling like one object is that the texture comes back. Not continuously, and not as atmosphere, but as a recurring band that carries the statements while the flat sections carry the reading.

The same material, a different grain — loud enough to mark the section, quiet enough to read across.

Why the band drops the highlight

The hero carries a radial highlight in its top-left corner, which is what gives it depth. Measured, that highlight makes the hero's top edge 13 luma brighter than its bottom — fine for a panel with only one seam at the bottom.

A band has two seams. With the highlight, its top edge measured 93 luma and its bottom 80, so no single flat green could meet both cleanly. Without it, the two edges come within 4 of each other and one green serves every cut on the page.

So the highlight is a hero-only device. That is also the right call hierarchically: the panel you land on should be the most dramatic thing here, and the bands that follow should be quieter echoes of it.
